Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. When you use the app, we collect the expense records you enter — amount, category, date, and any description you add.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate your account, display your expenses and spending summaries, and let you sign in securely. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in Spendly's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before being saved. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Any third-party services used to run the app (such as hosting or infrastructure providers) only process data as needed to keep the service running, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
